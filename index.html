About Geez Labs

Building portfolio websites, landing page apps, and custom digital experiences.

Good design is invisible. It gets out of the way and lets the work do the talking.

Geez Labs began with a simple frustration: too much of the web looks the same, loads slowly, and forgets the person on the other side of the screen. So I started building the opposite — small, hand-coded sites. Every pixel earns its place.

Today Geez Labs stays deliberately small — because your project deserves full attention. That means you work directly with the person designing and building your site. No account managers. No hand-offs. No diluted vision.

Just considered, conversion-focused work that actually performs. Faster load times. Clearer messaging. More inquiries.

Our mission

To craft fast, beautiful, purpose-driven websites that help small businesses and individuals turn visitors into clients — and make great design feel effortless.

What we stand for

Core values that guide every build.

Clarity first

Strip away the noise so the message — and the call to action — lands instantly.

Speed & craft

Hand-coded and performance-obsessed. Fast sites rank higher and convert better.

Accessible by default

Semantic, inclusive, responsive. Sites that work for everyone, on every device.

True partnership

Direct, honest collaboration from first call to launch — and well beyond it.

What makes us different.

Most agencies template their way to a finish line. We don't. Every site is built from scratch around your goals, your audience, and the single action you most want a visitor to take.

  • Conversion-focused, clutter-free design
  • Hand-coded — fast, accessible, no bloat
  • Direct collaboration, no middle layers
  • You own everything — code, files, and full control, no lock-in
